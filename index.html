About

Arman Mohseni (Tehran, 1997) is an Iranian filmmaker whose works explore themes of memory, uncertainty, and collective anxiety. His films often drift between the real and the imagined, reflecting a quiet disorientation that echoes both personal and societal unease. Alongside his film work, he directs for brands and artists seeking a similar visual language.
