Other Side of The Earth

2023 — Short Film

Synopsis

Years after disappearing during an investigation, a detective is recovered and brought in for questioning. Through recorded notes and fragmented memories, he retraces his search for a missing woman—a search that led him into a mystery much larger than the case itself.
